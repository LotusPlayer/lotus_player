Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 • Developer: Souljalazian (LotusPlayer)

1. Overview

Lotus Player ("we", "our", or "the App") is an offline-focused music player designed to respect user privacy. We believe your personal audio library and listening habits belong entirely to you.

2. Information We Collect & Device Permissions

Lotus Player operates locally on your device. The app requests the following Android permissions strictly to deliver audio playback:

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / Storage Permissions: Used solely to index, query, and play your local audio tracks, albums, and playlists. Your audio files are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • INTERNET & NETWORK_STATE: Used to check for app updates via our GitHub release portal, fetch synchronized lyrics upon user request, and connect to Google Firebase for anonymized diagnostics.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Allows continuous audio playback and lock-screen notification controls when the app is in the background.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to display the active media playback notification.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may integrate the following third-party infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase: Used for core service stability and basic performance diagnostics. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: Serves the update metadata (version.json) without collecting personal data.

4. Data Security & Storage

All playback metrics, playlists, and settings are saved locally on your device's internal storage via SQLite / SharedPreferences. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.
